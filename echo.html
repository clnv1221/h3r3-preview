INTRODUCTION

BACKGROUND

In the two years following Story Shop, I experienced changes I had never anticipated and was left with regrets I never had the chance to express. I kept encountering unfamiliar versions of myself, trying to understand those moments when I felt lost. When language could no longer describe them precisely, those uncertainties, memories and emotions found another way to remain.

They stayed in melodies, in stories, and in the sounds played over and over late at night.

Those sounds eventually came together as ECHO.

— h3R3 刘清云

THE ALBUM STORY

After losing you completely, after you were no longer anywhere in this world, I travelled through the fractures between memory, dreams and emotion, meeting you again through countless cycles.

Each search seemed to ask the same question: do you still exist somewhere, in a corner that time has yet to erase?

Gradually, the boundaries between reality and memory began to blur. Fragments that should have faded were continually rearranged, magnified and prolonged.

With every backward glance, a world belonging only to us was created anew.

“I live in a world where you still exist.

I remain in a time and space where your echoes can always be heard.”

ECHO unfolds as a complete trilogy: “Denial and Descent,” “Searching and Reconstruction,” and “Immersion and Echoes.”

Emotion folds back upon itself through time.

At last, he reaches a world shaped by obsession and longing.

But is that place real, a dream, or an illusion from which he cannot awaken?

The answer is left to everyone who hears ECHO.

PRODUCTION NOTES

Listening comes before explanation.

Imagery drives emotion.

Sound becomes narrative.

In ECHO, the story is told through distilled language and carefully chosen words, while sound carries its imagery to the listener. Emotions that words cannot fully describe remain within melody, space and atmosphere, allowing listeners to discover feelings and images of their own.

Love, loss, obsession, collapse and the sensation of approaching the edge of control are broken down into melody, frequency and space, then reassembled into the sonic world of ECHO.

Here, sound does more than carry the story. Sound itself is the story.

Every sound on the album has a role of its own. Vocals are no longer confined to the lead melody: they can tear like a guitar, while a guitar can whisper like a human voice. Reverb, delay, distortion, slicing, ambient sounds and changes in frequency are more than production techniques. Together, they participate in the narrative and shape its emotions.

Before the lyrics reach the listener’s ears, emotion has already begun.

ECHO is a creative experiment by h3R3 刘清云. It also brings him back to a question that matters deeply:

What is truly my own at this moment?

He understands that some sounds will not be understood immediately, yet believes they will continue to be heard at some point in the future.

The album looks beyond love at its brightest, towards emotions that are more intense, more complex and harder to explain: the struggle between holding on and letting go, the wavering between reason and losing control, and the moments when one knows something has ended yet cannot stop searching.

Love is an entry point into the creative process, allowing music to carry a broader range of emotions and expression.

ECHO is an exploration of the boundaries between sound and emotion. Everything that words cannot fully express remains within the music, waiting to become an echo in someone else’s life.
