INTRODUCTION

h3R3 刘清云 is a Chinese singer-songwriter, music producer and mixing engineer with over four million followers on NetEase Cloud Music. His best-known releases include “He Was Just Passing By,” “Can't Forget You” and “Still Missing You.” In May 2026, his music reached 10 billion streams on the platform, with multiple singles each recording over 10 million saves.

Named a NetEase Musician Artist to Watch in 2021, he subsequently released Story Shop, Part One, which ranked among NetEase Cloud Music's most popular albums of 2022. His work encompasses songwriting, performance and music production, distinguished by a recognisable vocal style and emotionally driven compositions. His live career includes multiple sold-out dates on nationwide tours and solo arena concerts under the title If the World Ends.
